# 10. AI Modernization Advisor
## Purpose
Turn a fictional current-state application profile into a reviewable modernization assessment, target options, risks, and phased roadmap.
## Inputs
Technology, topology, data platform, hosting, release process, scale, integrations, operational maturity, pain points, constraints, and business outcomes.
## Workflow
Validate input; calculate deterministic risk signals; retrieve standards; generate options; compare tradeoffs; draft target architecture and roadmap; validate claims; require consultant review.
## Outputs
Current-state summary, risk matrix, complexity drivers, alternatives, target-state recommendation, phased roadmap, investment priorities, limitations, evidence map, approval status.
## Guardrails
No autonomous estimates presented as facts; assumptions are labeled; recommendations cite inputs/standards; customer-specific data is prohibited in this portfolio.
## Tests
Schema, deterministic scores, missing inputs, conflicting constraints, citation validation, injection attempts, timeout, rejection, and versioning.
