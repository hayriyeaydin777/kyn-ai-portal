# 02. Business Problem

Recovery readiness is often fragmented across documents, spreadsheets, tickets, runbooks, contacts, and manual reviews. Teams may not know whether objectives are complete, dependencies have owners, evidence is current, or findings are supported.

The portal centralizes a minimum readiness model, applies versioned deterministic rules, and creates an evidence-grounded consultant brief. AI summarizes and explains; deterministic services establish facts.

Hypotheses: structured profiles improve consistency; deterministic checks improve trust; generated briefs require evidence; side effects require human approval; one complete workflow is stronger than a broad chatbot.
